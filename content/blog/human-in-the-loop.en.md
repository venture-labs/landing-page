    text: "Human in the loop means a person remains part of the process in an AI system: the AI prepares, and a person reviews and approves before the result takes effect outside. Responsibility stays with a person, not with the machine."
  - type: "heading"
    text: "When human in the loop applies and when it does not"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "An agent that delivers drafts someone approves works with human in the loop. An agent that sends, books or deletes by itself works without a person in the process. In between is monitoring after the fact: a person sees what happened but cannot intervene beforehand. The more expensive or irreversible a mistake, the more the approval belongs before the effect."

  - type: "paragraph"
