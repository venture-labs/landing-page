  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "In AI automation for mid-sized companies, handling receipts and managing email is often the first sensible use case. What matters is the division of labour: the agent reads, sorts and makes proposals. People decide on everything that moves money, goes outside the company or cannot be undone. What is certain runs automatically; what is uncertain becomes visible and waits for a short answer."
  - type: "heading"
    text: "Why receipts and email are a good fit"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "In both cases material arrives daily that someone must look at, classify and pass on: invoices, bank statements, contracts, enquiries, newsletters, notifications. The classification often follows rules and takes a lot of time without being difficult. That makes these tasks suitable for an AI agent that takes over a work step rather than just answering questions."
  - type: "heading"
    text: "Where the agent decides: sorting and assigning"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "A mail agent reads every new email, assigns a category and a project, and tags it. It does not send, does not reply and does not delete. Newsletters and notifications from known senders it can clear from the inbox, but only there, and everything stays retrievable through a tag. Anything that needs a reply or is urgent it leaves alone. Important items are reported at once, the rest as one list in the morning."  # prüfen: Own operation, status 04.11.2026 per the idea list “Mail sorter only reads” (mailroom: category, project, retrievability, list in the morning); Christian confirms description and status date, no sender or customer names
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "Receipts work similarly. In our operation an agent recognises the document type, such as invoice, bank statement or lease, and the assignment: private or business, and which business. That is routine work where a distinction takes seconds and where a person would otherwise pick up every slip of paper."  # prüfen: Own operation, status 08.11.2026 per the idea list “Private or business? How our agent sorts receipts”; Christian confirms status and flow, no company or person names
  - type: "heading"
    text: "Where the agent only proposes: making the uncertain visible"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "What happens under uncertainty is decisive. Only a confidently recognised financial document with a clearly assigned company goes into accounting. Everything unclear lands in a queue where a person decides with a short answer: business A, business B, private, not a receipt. Good automation does not mean doing everything automatically. It means doing the certain automatically and showing the uncertain."  # prüfen: Own operation, status 08.11.2026 per the idea list “Private or business?” (only confidently recognised receipts with a clear company go into accounting, unclear ones wait for an answer); Christian confirms the rule
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "The same applies to email. For preparing replies, a second agent can create a draft for a specific email. The draft is a proposal, not a finished result. It sits in the drafts folder, and a person reads it and sends it."
  - type: "heading"
    text: "What people decide"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "Three kinds of decisions stay with people, regardless of how well the agent sorts."
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "Money: payments, bookings with financial effect, refunds, contracts. The agent can prepare what needs checking, but a person gives the approval."
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "Sending: anything that leaves the company, meaning emails to customers, applicants or partners. At most the agent writes drafts. This applies especially to conflicts, contracts and money matters, which should be written personally anyway."
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "Deleting: anything that cannot be undone. Where agents tidy up, it happens in a way that lets every action be reversed, such as moving or tagging instead of deleting."
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "Behind this split is a simple consideration: a mistake in pre-sorting is annoying, a mistake in a payment or a sent email is no longer. The agent's permissions are therefore cut so that it may read and prepare but cannot trigger anything at the expensive points."
  - type: "heading"
    text: "Limits"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "The agent sorts by rules and a language model and is sometimes wrong. That is why it leaves alone anything that needs a reply, and why whatever it has moved stays retrievable. In our operation such a flow also runs only for selected mail types and document types, not for everything."  # prüfen: Own operation, status 04.11.2026: “sometimes wrong”, drafts only for selected mail types; Christian confirms which mail and document types may be named publicly and whether this is still true today
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "Also, what the agent sorts depends on what it may read. Which accounts and folders are connected and which data it sees is decided beforehand, as is what happens on an error and who notices it. Running such an agent needs someone responsible. And with personal data in emails and receipts, data protection questions are settled before the build, not after."  # prüfen: Data protection note kept general, no GDPR assurance; legal review of the wording before go-live, as for the landing page on AI agents for companies
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "A regular spot check belongs here. While an agent is new, it pays to verify part of its assignments by hand: are category and project right? Do the right receipts end up in the queue? If over several weeks it proves reliable for one kind of item, its room for manoeuvre there can grow. If the opposite shows, the rule is adjusted or the case narrowed again. Trust then comes from observation, not hope."
  - type: "heading"
    text: "How to get started"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "A good start is a short list: which three kinds of email and which kinds of receipt cost the most time? That is the rule list for the first agent. Added to it is what the agent may read, what it may tag, what only a person may do, and who works through the queue for unclear items. With this split a company can start small and extend later. A Pulse Check clarifies where the first case lies."
