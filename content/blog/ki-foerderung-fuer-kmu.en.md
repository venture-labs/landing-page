  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "Anyone looking for AI funding for SMEs meets recurring conditions. Usually the application must be filed before the project starts; even a signed contract can count as the start.   - type: "heading"
    text: "NRW.BANK.Impuls KI (North Rhine-Westphalia)"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "The programme is aimed at small and medium-sized enterprises under the EU definition with a seat in North Rhine-Westphalia. There are two modules: conceptual consulting, for example on finding use cases, setting goals and responsible use of AI, and the creation and testing of an AI system version for a concrete use case, where mainly external consulting and testing services are funded."  # prüfen: source https://www.nrwbank.de/impuls-ki (NRW.BANK), retrieved 09.11.2026; modules A and B and target group match the page
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "According to NRW.BANK, the grant is non-repayable and amounts to 50 percent of eligible costs, at most 25,000 euros within two years; the minimum size is 5,000 euros. The project should last no longer than six months and may only start after the grant notice. A company can be funded at most twice within two years."  # prüfen: rate, maximum, minimum threshold, duration, start after notice and twice rule per https://www.nrwbank.de/impuls-ki, retrieved 09.11.2026
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "The current status matters: on 09.11.2026, according to the page, further applications were not possible due to high demand, and the portal was closed.   - type: "heading"
    text: "BAFA: funding of business consulting for SMEs (nationwide)"
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "The federal programme subsidises consulting for small and medium-sized enterprises as a non-repayable grant. The application is made online, and consulting may only begin after a letter of information has been received; a signed consulting contract already counts as the start. Per company, at most five consultations within the validity period and at most two per year are eligible."  # prüfen: source BAFA programme page "Förderung von Unternehmensberatungen für KMU" (bafa.de, business consulting section), retrieved 09.11.2026; grant, application before start, contract counts as start, five/two consultations per the page
  - type: "paragraph"
    text: "The guideline applies to applications from 01.01.2023 and, according to BAFA, is extended with changed conditions for applications from 01.01.2027 to 31.12.2028. From 01.01.2027, applications are to be possible only via ELSTER or BundID. Funding rate and maximum amount are not on the programme page but in the guideline; they are deliberately not stated here. Whether a planned AI consultation falls under the eligible consulting topics must be clarified with BAFA beforehand."  # prüfen: term, extension to 31.12.2028, ELSTER/BundID from 01.01.2027 per BAFA page, retrieved 09.11.2026, and IHK Heilbronn-Franken of 02.11.2026 (quotes a BAFA press release of 30.10.2026).
